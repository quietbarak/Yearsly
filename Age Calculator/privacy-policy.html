Privacy Policy

How we protect your privacy and data at Yearsly.

1. Introduction

At Yearsly, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use our website.

2. Data Collection

Our calculators run entirely in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit your date of birth or any personal information you enter into the calculators.

3. Third-Party Services

We may use Google AdSense and Google Analytics to display ads and understand website usage. These services may collect anonymous data as outlined in their respective privacy policies.

4. Your Rights

Since we do not store personal data, there is no personal information to access, correct, or delete. For questions about third-party data, please contact those providers directly.

5. Contact

If you have privacy questions, please email us at support@agecalc.com.
