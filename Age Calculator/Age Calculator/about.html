About Us

Providing free, accurate, and fast age-related calculators.

Our Mission

Yearsly was created to provide a simple, fast, and completely free way to calculate exact age in years, months, and days. We believe everyone should have access to accurate date and age calculations without any cost.

Why We Made This Tool

Many people need to know their exact age for forms, legal documents, or just out of curiosity. Our calculator handles leap years and varying month lengths to give you the most precise result possible.

Our Promise

We will always keep Yearsly free, fast, and ad-light. Your privacy is important to us, and we never store your personal data.
